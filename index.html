Unsendly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Unsendly saves copies of incoming WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business messages from their notifications, so you can still read a message after the sender deletes it. Everything stays on your phone.

What the app accesses

  • Notifications, with your permission, through Android's notification access. Only notifications from WhatsApp (com.whatsapp) and WhatsApp Business (com.whatsapp.w4b) are processed. All other notifications are ignored and never stored.
  • Photo previews that WhatsApp attaches to a notification, when available.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock, only if you turn on App lock. Android checks it; Unsendly never sees or stores biometric data.

What is stored, and where

Sender name, chat name, message text, time and photo previews are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

What is shared

Nothing. Unsendly has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere. There are no ads, analytics or crash-reporting services. Data leaves your device only if you use "Export as text" and choose where to share it.

Deleting your data

Saved messages stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete a single chat, or everything, at any time in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

Children

Unsendly is not directed at children under 13.
